# Taxiopolis

![banner](/docs/img/banner-title.png)

Taxiopolis is a low-poly arcade taxi game inspired by classic arcade driving games like Crazy Taxi. Pick up passengers, race through a lively city, and deliver them to their destinations before time runs out. Drive faster to earn more money!

**Play the game:** https://mustafahtp.itch.io/taxiopolis  
**Gameplay trailer:** https://youtu.be/38D0ls1BXWo

## About the Project

The project started as an exploration of custom vehicle physics and gradually grew into a complete arcade driving game.

The project includes several systems developed from scratch, including vehicle physics, road tools, traffic AI, passenger and pedestrian systems, and gameplay abilities.

## Features

- Arcade-style vehicle physics and driving
- Custom road system and editor tools
- Traffic AI and pedestrian systems
- Passenger pickup and drop-off missions
- Time-based gameplay and fare rewards
- Driving combos, including drifting, airtime, and near misses
- Special abilities to help navigate traffic
- A low-poly city designed for exploration and fast-paced driving
